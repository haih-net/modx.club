MODX Project Audit

The audit isn't just for a fancy PDF. It is needed to understand what exactly is keeping the project in legacy and where the real risks lie.

We check:

  • MODX and PHP versions;
  • pdoTools, miniShop, and other components;
  • custom snippets, plugins, processors, and models;
  • data and resource structure;
  • dependencies between the frontend and MODX runtime;
  • server, environment, backups, and deployment;
  • URLs, SEO-critical elements, and external integrations.

The output is a project map: what can be kept, what needs to be stabilized, what is hindering development, and in what order it is safest to modify the system.
