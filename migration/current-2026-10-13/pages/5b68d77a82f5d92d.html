MODX Support and Modernization Services

We work exclusively with already existing MODX projects.

If a website brings revenue to your business but has become complex, fragile, or dependent on an old stack, the goal is not to patch it with the same tools for another ten years. First, the project needs to be brought back under control, and then the legacy limitations must be gradually removed.

Main areas of work:

  • MODX Website Support — ongoing technical maintenance, fixes, updates, infrastructure, and project health monitoring.
  • Customization of an Existing MODX Website — new functionality, integrations, APIs, automation, and business logic modifications without pointlessly rewriting the entire project.
  • Fixing and Restoring a MODX Website — diagnostics and troubleshooting of errors in old code, components, PHP, server environment, and their interactions.
  • MODX Project Audit — analysis of architecture, dependencies, infrastructure, data, and risks prior to major work.
  • MODX and PHP Upgrade — secure transition from older versions with compatibility checks for components and custom code.
  • Gradual Website Migration from MODX — transitioning the project to a modern stack in parts, without stopping business operations or losing SEO rankings.
  • Headless MODX and Modern Frontend — separating the frontend from the MODX runtime and connecting a modern stack via API.

We do not create new websites on MODX. Our task is different: take over an existing project, relieve the owner of technical headaches, and gradually free the business from legacy dependency.
