MODX Website Support

If a MODX website is already up and running and generating revenue for your business, it shouldn't be turned into an endless experiment with changing contractors.

We take on the entire technical maintenance of your project: we dive into the current architecture, dependencies, and infrastructure, fix issues, manage updates, and steadily reduce technical debt.

Support may include:

  • bug fixing;
  • development of existing features;
  • updating MODX, PHP, and components;
  • server and environment management;
  • backup and disaster recovery;
  • performance and stability monitoring;
  • preparing the project for future upgrades.

Our goal is not just to be an "on-call programmer," but to restore predictability and control over the website for its owner.
